5 FP&A Practices That Drive Executive Decisions

By Alberto Ramirez Huaman · Finance Leader

In high-growth and multi-jurisdictional environments, FP&A is no longer a back-office reporting function—it is a strategic partner to the C-suite. After more than a decade leading FP&A and commercial finance across telecom and technology, here are five practices that consistently improve decision quality:

  1. Integrated P&L, Cash Flow & Scenario Models — Single-source models that link operational drivers to financial outcomes enable rapid “what-if” analysis and protect margins under currency or volume volatility.
  2. Channel & SKU-Level Profitability — Moving beyond aggregate margins to granular channel and product profitability surfaces pricing and promotional opportunities that can lift gross margin by 1%+.
  3. Make-vs-Buy & TCO Discipline — Rigorous total-cost-of-ownership analysis for sourcing decisions has delivered measurable margin gains while safeguarding supply resilience.
  4. Variance Analysis Tied to Action — Price, yield, and efficiency variances only create value when they trigger corrective actions owned by commercial and operations teams.
  5. Cross-Border Governance as a Margin Lever — Transfer pricing, tax coordination, and currency risk management are not compliance checkboxes—they are active tools to protect consolidated profitability.

These practices, applied consistently, have supported $50M+ portfolio decisions, multi-million-dollar cost optimizations, and clearer long-range planning conversations with senior leadership.
